We published a deep dive into a problem we hear about constantly: the hidden cost of manual spreadsheet work in marketing reporting.

The headline numbers are hard to ignore — marketing teams spend an average of 14.5 hours per week collecting and preparing data (Treasure Data global survey). Add the 10+ hours per week on manual campaign optimization, and your best people are spending the equivalent of days per week producing zero strategic output.

The research is blunt about the price: 78% of marketing decision-makers believe at least 10% of spend is wasted due to insufficient measurement (Haus, 2026). Organizations waste an average of 25% of their marketing budget on efforts that look productive but do not drive revenue (DemandScience, 2026). And it gets worse:

  • Only 49% of marketing and finance leaders can clearly explain how marketing is measured to the board (Haus, 2026)
  • One-third of CMOs rely on 5 to 15 tools to measure ROI, and 54% say connecting data from different sources is a major barrier (NIQ 2026 CMO Outlook)
  • Marketers spend over 10 hours per week on manual campaign optimization, costing North American agencies about $17,000 per employee per year (DoubleVerify, 2025)

The article covers why adding headcount will not fix a broken pipeline and the 3-week timeline to eliminate the hidden tax for good.
