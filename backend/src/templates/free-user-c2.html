Most marketing teams rely on spreadsheets or platform-specific dashboards to report on performance. The problem? These tools weren't built to answer the questions your CEO and CFO are actually asking. Here are three gaps that hold teams back:

  1. Cross-source correlation — Your Google Ads data lives in one place, your CRM in another, your analytics platform somewhere else. Piecing them together manually takes hours and introduces errors. You need a unified view to understand which campaigns are actually driving pipeline.
  2. Real-time vs retrospective — By the time your monthly spreadsheet is ready, the data is already stale. You're making decisions on last month's numbers when you need to react to this week's performance. The gap between what's happening and what you can see is costing you.
  3. Narrative not numbers — A table of numbers doesn't tell a story. Your CFO doesn't want raw data — they want a clear answer: "Are we spending efficiently? Which channels are working? What should we do next?" Turning data into a narrative is the hardest part of reporting.
